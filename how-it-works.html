How it works

Three steps. Free for homeowners. No obligation.

1. Tell us what you need

Use our short request form. You’ll pick a service type (repair, replace, install, tune-up, or “I’m not sure”), tell us how urgent it is, and optionally share your system’s age. Then add your ZIP, contact details, and the time of day that works best for you.

2. We match licensed shops

We send your request to licensed Utah HVAC contractors who serve your area. We only work with Utah-licensed shops; see contractor partners. We don’t write quotes or diagnose your system. That’s the contractor’s job.

3. You choose your next step

Talk with the shops, compare what they offer, and hire who you want, or no one. There’s no fee and no obligation. You can opt out at any time; see our TCPA disclosure.

What it costs

Nothing for homeowners. Contractors pay us per lead. Details: How we make money.
