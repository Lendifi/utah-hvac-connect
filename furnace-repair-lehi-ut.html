What the numbers suggest for furnace calls

This is general context, not a diagnosis of your furnace:

  • Homes built in the 2000s (35%) may still have original furnaces that are now roughly 17 to 26 years old. Furnace makers commonly cite a 15-to-20-year typical lifespan (American Standard), so repair-or-replace questions are common here. Our replacement guide lists what to ask.
  • Homes built in 2010 or later (42%) have furnaces that are often under 16 years old. Before approving a repair on a newer system, ask the shop to check whether the furnace or the failed part is still under manufacturer warranty, and find your registration paperwork if you have it.
  • 82% of occupied homes heat with utility gas, so most no-heat calls in Lehi involve a gas furnace. If you ever smell gas, leave first, then call.

Lehi’s heating season

Utah County winters are close to Salt Lake’s. At the Provo BYU weather station, NOAA’s 1991–2020 normals show about 4,968 heating degree days a year, climbing from 327 in October to 995 in December and 1,007 in January (NOAA monthly normals; annual). Provo is the Utah County station we used, so treat it as a regional guide rather than a reading for your street.

Shops get busiest in December and January. If your furnace is making new noises or short-cycling in October or November, getting it looked at before the first deep cold is easier than finding an opening during it.

Utilities and rebates in Lehi

  • Electric: Lehi runs its own Power Department. Its energy conservation and rebates page lists rebate information for new air conditioners and points to gas-utility rebates for furnaces. Rocky Mountain Power’s Wattsmart program applies only to Rocky Mountain Power customers, so check whose name is on your electric bill.
  • Gas: Enbridge Gas ThermWise listed $300 for a 95%–97.4% AFUE gas furnace and $350 for 97.5% AFUE or higher with an ECM blower on October 7, 2026, with the request due within six months of installation (ThermWise).
  • Federal: the 25C tax credit isn’t allowed for equipment placed in service after December 31, 2025 (IRS).

Permits in Lehi

Lehi’s Building and Inspections Department issues building permits in the city through its online portal and schedules inspections by phone. A furnace or AC replacement normally needs a permit. Ask your contractor whether the permit is included and who schedules the inspection.

Check the license before work starts

Before anyone works on your system, look the company up on DOPL’s free Licensee Lookup & Verification. You want an Active status and an HVAC classification (H100, or the older S350), under the same business name that’s on your estimate. Step-by-step: how to check a Utah HVAC license.
