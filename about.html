About us

A Utah-based matchmaker for homeowners and licensed HVAC shops

Utah HVAC Connect is a Utah-based matchmaking service. We connect homeowners in Salt Lake County and Utah County with licensed Utah HVAC contractors who serve their area.

What we do

You tell us what you need (a repair, a replacement, an install, a tune-up, or “I’m not sure”). We pass your request to licensed local shops so they can follow up about it. You decide whether to hire anyone.

What we don’t do

We are not an HVAC contractor. We don’t perform HVAC work, install equipment, give quotes, or send technicians. Pricing and proposals come from the contractors, not from us.

Who we are

Utah HVAC Connect is an Apex Lead Group, LLC company. It’s free for homeowners; contractors pay us per lead. Read how we make money.
