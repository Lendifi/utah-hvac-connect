How we share information

When you submit a request, we share your request details and contact info with matched licensed HVAC contractors so they can contact you about it. Contractors pay us per lead, so under some state privacy laws this may count as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information.

Opt out or request deletion

Email hello@lendifi.io (subject: “Do Not Sell or Share Request”) to opt out of future sharing or to ask us to delete your information. Include the name, email, or phone number you used so we can find your request.

Global Privacy Control

It is our policy to honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
