Is Utah HVAC Connect legit?

Honest answers to fair questions

Who runs it?

Utah HVAC Connect is operated by Apex Lead Group, LLC. You can reach us at hello@utahhvacconnect.com.

How do you make money?

Contractors pay us per lead. Homeowners pay nothing, and we never take a cut of your job or mark up prices. See How we make money.

Are the contractors licensed?

We only partner with Utah-licensed HVAC contractors. You can check any license yourself through the Utah DOPL public lookup; see Licenses.

Am I obligated to hire anyone?

No. Sending a request is free, and you’re never required to hire anyone.

Are you an HVAC contractor?

No. We’re a matchmaker. We don’t perform HVAC work, give quotes, or dispatch technicians.

How do I opt out or delete my info?

Reply STOP to any text from a contractor, or email hello@utahhvacconnect.com. For data requests, see Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information.
