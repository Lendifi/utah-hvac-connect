Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026 (draft)

Draft stub. This is a placeholder privacy page for the site draft. Have counsel review before launch. Brand name: Utah HVAC Connect.

Utah HVAC Connect is operated by Apex Lead Group, LLC (“we,” “us”), which runs a matchmaking / lead-generation service that connects Utah homeowners with licensed HVAC contractors. We are not an HVAC contractor, do not perform HVAC work, and do not provide quotes.

Information we collect

When you submit a match request, we may collect:

  • Service details (type, urgency, optional system age)
  • Location (ZIP, optional city)
  • Whether you are the homeowner
  • Contact info (first name, last name, mobile, email)
  • Preferred contact window
  • TCPA / SMS consent status and timestamp

We do not ask for date of birth, income, Social Security number, payment card details, or utility account numbers.

How we use information

  • To match you with licensed HVAC contractors serving your area
  • To allow those contractors to contact you about your request (with your consent)
  • To operate, improve, and secure our service
  • To respond to inquiries from homeowners or contractor partners

Sharing with contractors

When you submit a request and provide consent, we share your request details with one or more licensed HVAC contractors so they can call or text you about that request. We do not sell leads to non-HVAC marketers for unrelated promotions.

Do Not Sell / Share (stub)

Do Not Sell My Personal Information. Depending on applicable law (including state privacy statutes), “sale” or “sharing” may include disclosing personal information for valuable consideration. Lead matching may fall under those definitions.

To exercise Do Not Sell / Share or other privacy rights, email samueljohnsen3@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request” and enough detail for us to locate your information. We will respond as required by law. See Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information for details, including how we honor Global Privacy Control signals.

SMS / calls

By checking the consent box on our form, you agree that licensed HVAC contractors may call or text you about your request at the number you provide. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase. You may opt out of texts by replying STOP to the sender.
