Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how this service works.

Are you an HVAC contractor?
No. We match homeowners with licensed Utah HVAC contractors. We do not perform HVAC work, install equipment, give quotes, or send our own technicians.
Is this really free?
Yes for homeowners. There’s no fee to submit a request. Contractors who receive leads pay us — you don’t.
Do you give quotes or help me compare prices?
No. We’re a matchmaker. We introduce you to licensed local pros; pricing and proposals come from those shops, not from us.
What areas do you cover?
Primary focus is Salt Lake County and Utah County, with expansion across the Wasatch Front as contractor partners grow. Enter your ZIP on the request form to check.
Do I have to hire someone?
No. You’re free to talk with matched shops, wait, or decline — no purchase required and consent to contact is not a condition of purchase.
