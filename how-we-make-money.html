How we make money

Plain answers about our business model

Homeowners pay nothing

Sending a request is free. We never charge homeowners a fee.

Contractors pay per lead

Licensed HVAC contractors pay us for each request they receive. A lead can be shared (sent to more than one shop) or exclusive (sent to one shop).

What we never do

  • We never take a cut of the job you hire a contractor for.
  • We never mark up contractor prices. Pricing comes straight from the shop you choose.
  • We don’t sell your request to non-HVAC marketers for unrelated promotions. See our privacy policy.

Contractors interested in leads can visit For contractors.
