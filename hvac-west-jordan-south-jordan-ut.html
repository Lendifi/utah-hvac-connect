What the numbers suggest

General context, not a diagnosis:

  • 84081 (west West Jordan): the 2000s-era share here (37%) is the largest of any ZIP on our local pages. Furnaces original to those homes are now roughly 17 to 26 years old, past the 15-to-20-year span furnace makers commonly cite (American Standard). Bring our repair-or-replace questions to the shop.
  • 84009 (South Jordan, including Daybreak): mostly built in 2010 or later. Ask about manufacturer warranty before approving repairs on newer systems.
  • 84095 (east South Jordan): 41% of units predate 2000, so some homes are on a second furnace and AC, and some still have originals. If both are the same age, ask about replacing them together; see AC and heat pump options.

Heating season

At Salt Lake City International Airport, NOAA’s 1991–2020 normals show about 5,154 heating degree days a year, from 337 in October to 1,018 in December and 1,041 in January (NOAA monthly normals; annual). The south valley and benches sit higher than the airport, so treat the airport as a regional guide rather than a reading for your street.

Utilities and rebates

  • Gas: Enbridge Gas ThermWise listed $300 for a 95%–97.4% AFUE gas furnace and $350 for 97.5% AFUE or higher with an ECM blower, plus $700–$1,200 for qualifying dual-fuel systems, on October 7, 2026 (ThermWise).
  • Electric: if your bill says Rocky Mountain Power, Wattsmart listed up to $1,450 for an AHRI-rated dual-fuel heat pump system ($700 if not AHRI-rated), installed by a participating contractor in an existing single-family home (Wattsmart).
  • Federal: the 25C tax credit isn’t allowed for equipment placed in service after December 31, 2025 (IRS).

Permits

West Jordan’s published monthly permit reports list furnace and air-conditioner permits (example report); its Building & Safety division takes applications online. In South Jordan, start with the city’s Building Department. Ask your contractor whether the permit is in the bid.

Check the license before work starts

Before anyone works on your system, look the company up on DOPL’s free Licensee Lookup & Verification. You want an Active status and an HVAC classification (H100, or the older S350), under the same business name that’s on your estimate. Step-by-step: how to check a Utah HVAC license.

FAQ

Does Daybreak count as South Jordan?

Yes. Daybreak is a community in South Jordan, and ZIP 84009 includes it. Enter your ZIP on the Get Help form.

My West Jordan home was built in the early 2000s and the furnace is original. What should I ask?

Ask what failed, whether there’s any safety concern, whether parts are available, and what repair costs compared with replacement, in writing. If replacement comes up, ask about venting changes, permit, rebates, and who files the paperwork.

Do I need a permit to replace a furnace in West Jordan or South Jordan?

Normally, yes. West Jordan’s permit reports list furnace and AC permits, and South Jordan’s Building Department handles permits there. Your contractor usually pulls it.

Which rebates apply here?

Enbridge Gas ThermWise for qualifying gas furnaces and dual-fuel systems; Wattsmart dual-fuel rebates if you’re a Rocky Mountain Power customer. The federal 25C credit ended for equipment placed in service after December 31, 2025.

Is the request free, and do you send the technician?

The request is free for homeowners with no obligation to hire. We don’t send technicians or quote; we’re a matchmaker that connects you with licensed Utah HVAC shops that serve your ZIP. Contractors who receive leads pay us.
