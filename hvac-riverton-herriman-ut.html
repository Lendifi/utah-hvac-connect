What the numbers suggest

General context, not a diagnosis:

  • Riverton/Bluffdale (84065): with a median build year of 2002 and nearly half of units built before 2000, many homes have furnaces and ACs that are original or on their second set. Furnace makers commonly cite a 15-to-20-year typical lifespan (American Standard), so expect repair-or-replace questions. Start with our replacement questions.
  • Herriman (84096): more than half of units were built in 2010 or later, so many systems are under 16 years old. Before approving a repair, ask whether the equipment or part is still under manufacturer warranty. The oldest Herriman subdivisions are now reaching the age where first replacements come up.
  • Heating fuel: 90% of occupied homes in 84065 and 82% in 84096 heat with utility gas.

Heating season in the southwest valley

At Salt Lake City International Airport, NOAA’s 1991–2020 normals show about 5,154 heating degree days a year, from 337 in October to 1,018 in December and 1,041 in January (NOAA monthly normals; annual). The south valley and benches sit higher than the airport, so treat the airport as a regional guide rather than a reading for your street.

Utilities and rebates

  • Gas: Enbridge Gas ThermWise listed $300 for a 95%–97.4% AFUE gas furnace and $350 for 97.5% AFUE or higher with an ECM blower, plus $700–$1,200 for qualifying dual-fuel systems, on October 7, 2026 (ThermWise).
  • Electric: if your bill says Rocky Mountain Power, Wattsmart listed up to $1,450 for an AHRI-rated dual-fuel heat pump system ($700 if not AHRI-rated), installed by a participating contractor in an existing single-family home (Wattsmart).
  • Federal: the 25C tax credit isn’t allowed for equipment placed in service after December 31, 2025 (IRS).

Permits

Furnace and AC replacements normally need a permit and inspection from your city: Riverton Building Inspections and Permits or Herriman Building Permits. Bluffdale addresses go through Bluffdale City. Ask your contractor whether the permit is in the bid.

Check the license before work starts

Before anyone works on your system, look the company up on DOPL’s free Licensee Lookup & Verification. You want an Active status and an HVAC classification (H100, or the older S350), under the same business name that’s on your estimate. Step-by-step: how to check a Utah HVAC license.

FAQ

Is Bluffdale covered?

Yes, as part of our Salt Lake County focus. ZIP 84065 covers Riverton and parts of Bluffdale. Enter your ZIP on the Get Help form; matching depends on licensed shops that serve it.

My Herriman home is less than 15 years old. Is it worth repairing the furnace?

We don’t make that call, and age alone doesn’t decide it. On a newer system, ask the shop to check manufacturer warranty coverage for the furnace and the failed part before you approve a repair.

My Riverton furnace is original to a 2000s home. What should I ask?

Ask what failed, whether parts are readily available, whether there’s any safety concern, and what repair costs compared with replacement, in writing. Our furnace replacement guide lists more questions, plus 2026 rebates.

Which city handles HVAC permits?

Your own city: Riverton’s Building department, Herriman’s Building Permits office, or Bluffdale City. Most homeowners have the contractor pull the permit.
