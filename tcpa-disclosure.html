TCPA disclosure

Calls and texts about your request

Draft. Not attorney-reviewed.

The consent you give

The checkbox on our request form reads, word for word:

“Yes, licensed HVAC contractors may call or text me about this request at the number I provided. Msg/data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See our Privacy Policy.”

What it means

  • Licensed HVAC contractors matched to your request may call or text you about it at the number you provided.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase. You don’t have to agree in order to buy any goods or services.
  • Message and data rates may apply.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any text to stop texts from that sender, or email hello@lendifi.io and we’ll stop sharing your request.
