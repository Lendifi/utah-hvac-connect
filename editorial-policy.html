Editorial policy

How we write seasonal and local content

  • Factual and sourced. Claims about weather, climate, population, or rules link to the primary source (for example NOAA/NWS, Utah DOPL, or the Kem C. Gardner Policy Institute).
  • No paid placements disguised as advice. Contractors can’t pay to be recommended in our articles. If we ever publish sponsored content, it will be clearly labeled.
  • Cost figures are cited or omitted. We don’t publish price ranges we can’t source. Your actual price comes from the contractor.
  • Not legal, medical, or safety advice. Our content is general information. If you smell gas or suspect carbon monoxide, leave the home and call 911 or your gas utility.
  • Corrections. Spot an error? Email samueljohnsen3@gmail.com and we’ll review and fix it.
